## See your dermatologist instead of experimenting more if
- More than **10 palms** of skin are affected, or it's on the face, genitals, hands, feet or nails and bothering you.
- Psoriasis is seriously affecting daily life, sleep, work or mood.
- **Any joint symptoms.**
- **No improvement after 8–12 weeks** of using treatment correctly.
- You need a strong steroid continuously to stay clear.
